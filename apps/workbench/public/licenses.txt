Rhyme Workbench — third-party data and font notices
=====================================================

Rhyme Workbench ships three third-party datasets and one font inside the app.
Their licences require the notices below to travel with any redistribution,
which includes the built app. Everything else — the phonetic engine, the slang
and curated pronunciation supplement, the scoring and the UI — is Rhyme
Workbench's own.

3. Word frequency ranks
-----------------------
The frequency ranks inside public/data/lexicon.txt are the word order of
Peter Norvig's count_1w.txt (https://norvig.com/ngrams/), itself derived from
the Google Web Trillion Word Corpus. Only each word's rank position is used.
