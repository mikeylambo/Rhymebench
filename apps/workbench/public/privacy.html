Privacy

Rhyme Workbench · last updated 24 September 2026

Short version: Rhyme Workbench collects nothing. Everything you type and save stays on your device.

What happens to what you write

Every rhyme lookup, definition, generated line and analysis runs in your browser, on your device. The words and lines you type are never sent to a server. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync.

What is stored, and where

Your pins, rhyme families, schemes, rhyme chain, Scratchpad text, current Write line and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on this device, so they are there next time. Nobody else, including us, can read them. Clearing your browser's site data for this app deletes them. You can export them any time from Data & about → Download backup, and restore them the same way.

So that the app works offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the app itself and its dictionary files in its cache. These contain nothing of yours.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no advertising, and no third-party scripts. The app loads its fonts and data from its own site, not from any outside service.

Like any website, the server that hosts the app may keep standard short-lived request logs (for example IP addresses and request times) for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify or track anyone.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does anything differently.
